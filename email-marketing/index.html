Common questions from IT and compliance teams.

Degaussing uses a powerful magnetic field to erase the magnetic domains on media like HDDs and tapes, rendering the stored data unreadable while the drive itself stays intact. Physical destruction crushes, shreds, or punctures the media, including its internal controller and memory chips. SSDs and flash media store data electronically rather than magnetically, so they don't respond to degaussing and require physical destruction to guarantee unrecoverable data.

Yes. Fractalio performs data destruction directly at a customer's facility across India, so sensitive media never leaves the premises before it is destroyed. This closes the chain-of-custody gap that comes with shipping drives to an off-site facility.

Fractalio's destruction and documentation practices are designed to support the data sanitization and security-safeguard obligations referenced under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, as well as frameworks like ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS. Customers should confirm specifics with their own compliance and legal teams, since regulatory requirements vary by sector and jurisdiction.

Fractalio handles hard disk drives (HDD), solid-state drives (SSD), magnetic tape, flash media, and smartphones, using degaussing for magnetic media and physical shredding or crushing for SSDs and other non-magnetic media.

Destroyed media is processed through eco-friendly recycling practices as part of the same auditable workflow, so disposal is documented alongside the destruction event itself.
